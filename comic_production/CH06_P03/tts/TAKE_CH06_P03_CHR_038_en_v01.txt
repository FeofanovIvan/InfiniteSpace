But then we discovered strange natural anomalies.

To understand how they worked, we had to study the nature of the universe even more deeply.

While investigating one of these anomalies, we met your familiar magnetic beings.

And farther on, in the depths of space, we discovered an astonishing artifact.

It turned out to be the center of many anomalous zones.

We made the study of this complex artifact the purpose of our existence and built a station around it called “The Last Gates.”

Not long ago, a strange signal reached us — an echo of terrible events happening somewhere far away.
