Privacy Policy

Infinite Space · Effective November 18, 2026

Infinite Space is an educational space-adventure game for children and families. Protecting children and their families is central to its design.

1. Information we collect

Infinite Space does not require an account and does not ask children for their name, email address, phone number, precise location, photographs, contacts, or other personal information.

Game progress, preferences, earned points, free-play time, and full-game access status are stored locally on the device so the game can remember progress and access.

2. One-time purchase

In this Android release, the optional full-game unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Google Play. It is not a subscription and does not renew automatically. We do not receive or store payment-card details. The game receives limited information from Google Play, such as the product identifier and purchase status, to unlock or restore access. Google processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

3. Children's privacy

The game is intended for children and does not include advertising, social networking, public profiles, or third-party behavioural tracking. Purchase controls are placed behind a parental gate. A parent or guardian should make all purchase decisions.

4. Analytics and advertising

The current version does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not track children across apps or websites.

5. Sensors and device features

Some game activities may use the device accelerometer for local tilt controls. Sensor readings are used on the device and are not sent to the developer. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise-location access.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on the device until app data is cleared or the game is removed. Purchase records are retained by the relevant app store under its policies. Because Infinite Space does not operate user accounts or collect personal information on its own servers, there is no separate Infinite Space account record to delete.

7. Security and external links

Network access is limited to app-store billing and external legal pages. External links open outside the game.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
